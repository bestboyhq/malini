---
title: The window
description: One bar at the top of Malini's window on every OS, with the window controls at its left end, the menu behind one button on Windows and Linux, and the same shortcuts everywhere.
---

Malini's window has one bar at the top.
It holds the window controls, the sidebar and inspector buttons, and your pull request's status.
Drag an empty part of it to move the window, and double-click one to maximize the window or restore it.

## macOS

The traffic lights sit at the bar's left end, and Malini's menu is in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

## Windows

Close, minimize and maximize sit at the bar's left end, where the traffic lights sit on a Mac: red, yellow and green, and grey while another window is in front.
Point at them to see their symbols.
The green one maximizes the window, and restores it once it is maximized.
Its snap layouts do not open on hover; **Win+Z** opens them.
Malini has no menu bar.
**Menu**, the button beside the controls, opens the whole menu under it, and so does pressing **Alt** on its own.
The arrow keys move through it, and **Esc** closes it.

Every shortcut works without a menu bar:

- **Ctrl+N** is **New Workstream**, and **Ctrl+T** is **New Chat**.
- **Ctrl+O** is **Open Folder…**, and **Ctrl+,** is **Settings…**.
- **Ctrl+=** zooms in, **Ctrl+-** zooms out, and **Ctrl+0** goes back to 100%.
- **Ctrl+Z**, **Ctrl+X**, **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+V** and **Ctrl+A** edit text, as everywhere else.

## Linux

The window works as on Windows: the controls at the bar's left end, **Menu** and **Alt** for the menu, and the same shortcuts.
Malini draws the controls itself, in place of your desktop's title bar, so they sit at the left even where your desktop puts its own at the right.
The menu takes its colors from your desktop theme, not from Malini's.
Your dock, taskbar and window switcher show Malini's icon.
